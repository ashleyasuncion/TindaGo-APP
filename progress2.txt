WHAT WAS DONE IN TASK 1 (2 files, +7 lines total):
1) app/build.gradle.kts (+3 lines, after WorkManager block, lines 83-85):
     // Supabase sync (Phase 2 — Ktor wraps HttpURLConnection, no native code)
     implementation("io.ktor:ktor-client-core:2.3.12")
     implementation("io.ktor:ktor-client-android:2.3.12")
   Why Ktor: ktor-client-android wraps Android's built-in HttpURLConnection —
   safest engine (no OkHttp, no CIO DNS issues, zero native code). Only org.json
   + Room + WorkManager existed before; no JSON/HTTP lib. INTERNET perm was
   missing. Known risk: Ktor 2.3.12 targets Kotlin 1.9.x, project is 2.0.21 —
   watch resolution. Fallback: raw HttpURLConnection (~40 lines, zero deps).
2) app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml (+4 lines, after SEND_SMS, lines 14-16):
     <!-- Phase 2: cloud sync to Supabase (Ktor/HttpURLConnection + PostgREST/Auth) -->
     <uses-permission android:name="android.permission.INTERNET" />
     <uses-permission android:name="android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE" />
   Why both: INTERNET is mandatory (was missing); ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE enables
   a future offline-guard before syncNow() so taps fail fast with a message.
